About This Outfit

Chaser 605 is a just-for-fun site by Jeremy and Grant, a friendly tip of the cowboy hat to our storm-chasing buddy. Weather updates, pictures, videos, and good times. The 605 is South Dakota's area code, so that's where our weather lives.

⚡ All in fun! No tornadoes, chasers, or beverages were harmed in the making of this site. We're just a couple of friends who love weather.
